How to use your key
  1. Open your Roblox executor
  2. Paste and run: Goat_key = "YourKeyHere"
  3. Then execute the GoatAuth loader script
  4. The loader will authenticate you and load your script automatically
Loader Script
Lua 
Goat_key = "your-key-here"
loadstring(game:HttpGet("https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GoatHub/loader/main/loader.lua"))()

Replace your-key-here with your username/key.
